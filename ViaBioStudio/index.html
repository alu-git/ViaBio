About Us 关于我们

ViaBio Medical and its affiliated holding subsidiaries are a special purpose company (SPV) established in Yangzhou High-tech Zone, Jiangsu Province, supported by local government and venture capital (VC) partners. 威必翱医学及其附属控股子公司在上海浦江国际生命科技园设有创新中心，拥有1200平方的办公及研发实验室，在扬州设有2000多平方的 GMP 生产车间。
